# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

**[Your name / company]** ("we") built this app for families. This policy
explains how it handles information. The App Store requires a privacy policy,
and a kids' app especially — so host this text at a public URL and link it in
App Store Connect.

## The short version

We do not collect, see, store, sell, or share any personal information. The
app has no servers and no analytics.

## Where your information lives

- All settings and your child's progress are stored **on your devices** and
  synced through **your own Apple iCloud account** (Apple's iCloud Key-Value
  storage). We have no access to it.
- Contacts and phone numbers you enter stay on your devices and in your
  iCloud. They are used only so the watch can call or message those numbers.
- Voice notes recorded on the watch stay on the watch.
- Location is used **on the device** to calculate prayer times and Qibla, and
  to show the child in your own Find My. It is not sent to us.

## Children's privacy

This app is designed to be used by children under the supervision of a
parent, who sets it up and controls it. We do not knowingly collect any data
from anyone, including children. We show no third-party advertising and
include no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Apple services

Syncing uses Apple iCloud and, for calls and messages, your carrier. Their
handling of data is governed by Apple's and your carrier's privacy policies.

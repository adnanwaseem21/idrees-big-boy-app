## Description
A kind, simple Apple Watch app for your child — and a parent app to run it
from your phone.

On the watch, your child gets:
• Chores, routines and daily activities that earn points
• Rewards you choose, redeemed with your okay
• A daily schedule with gentle reminders
• One-tap calls and quick messages to you
• Voice notes
• A game of Snake you can lock during school and bedtime
• (Optional) Prayer times, Qibla, duas and a prayer streak

From your iPhone you control everything: add or change chores, set the
schedule, pick who they can call, choose rewards, turn features on or off, and
see how their week went.

Private by design: everything runs on the watch and syncs through your own
family iCloud. We collect nothing and have no servers. It all works offline;
only calls and texts use the watch's cellular signal.

Faith features are optional — turn them on for prayer times, Qibla, duas and
adhan, or leave them off for a simple kids' watch.

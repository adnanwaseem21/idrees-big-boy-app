Privacy Policy

Idrees Big Boy App · Last updated 20 October 2026

We do not collect, see, store, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no servers and no analytics.

Where your information lives

  • All settings and your child's progress are stored on your devices and synced through your own Apple iCloud account. We have no access to it.
  • Contacts and phone numbers you enter stay on your devices and in your iCloud. They are used only so the watch can call or message those numbers.
  • Voice notes recorded on the watch stay on the watch.
  • Location is used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla, and to show the child in your own Find My. It is never sent to us.

Children's privacy

This app is designed to be used by children under the supervision of a parent, who sets it up and controls it. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. We show no third-party advertising and include no third-party analytics or tracking.

Apple services & your carrier

Syncing uses Apple iCloud and, for calls and messages, your mobile carrier. Their handling of data is governed by Apple's and your carrier's own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Getting started

  • Install the app on your child's Apple Watch from the App Store on the watch (a Family Setup watch can do this on its own).
  • Install the parent app on your iPhone to manage chores, the schedule, rewards, who they can call, and prayer settings.
  • Everything runs offline and syncs through your own family iCloud — no account, no servers.

Common questions

Does my child need their own iPhone?

No. The watch app runs on its own. Set the watch up with Apple's Family Setup, paired to your iPhone.

Do I need a cellular plan on the watch?

For calling, messaging and location away from home, yes — a Family Setup watch uses its own cellular line. The rest of the app works offline.

Are the Islamic features required?

No. Prayer times, Qibla and duas are optional and can be turned off in the parent app for a simple kids' watch.

Where is my data?

On your devices and in your own iCloud. We collect nothing. See our Privacy Policy.
